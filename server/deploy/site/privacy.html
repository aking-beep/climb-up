CLIMB UP privacy policy

Effective: 15 October 2026
Publisher: ARC Transformation Group
Contact: aking@arctransformationgroup.com

CLIMB UP is in a closed beta. This policy covers the game for Android and its optional online records service.

The short version

  • You can play the whole game without an account. Your progress and records then stay on your device.
  • Online records are optional. They need an account, which you create in the game. It needs no email, no password and no real name.
  • We do not show ads. We do not sell or share your data, use it to track you across apps, or collect your location, contacts or photos.
  • You can delete your online account and everything it holds from inside the game at any time.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress, settings, personal records and a local usage log on your device. None of this is sent anywhere unless you create an online account. Even then only your finished climbs are sent (below), never the usage log. Uninstalling the game deletes it. Use "Delete my records on this device" on the records screen to clear your records sooner.

Our server logs each request with its time, the kind of request and whether it worked. The logs do not include your account code or your network address. To stop abuse, the server remembers which network addresses made new accounts in the last hour. It keeps this only in memory and never writes it to disk. Connections are encrypted (HTTPS).

Who can see your records

  • Adults: public by default. Your name and records can appear on the Global leaderboards.
  • 13 to 17: friends-only by default. Only players you add as friends see your name and records.
  • Under 13: online accounts are not available in the beta. Records stay on the device.

A first verified ascent stays in the record book. If you delete your account, it shows as "a deleted account".

Your choices and rights

  • Delete: Records → Friends or Global → My account → "Delete my online account". This removes your account, climbs, friends and reports from the service at once. Backups are kept for up to 14 days and then replaced.
  • Copy: we can send you a copy of everything the service holds about you (the service's /v1/me/export). Contact us with your recovery code.
  • Correct: change your public name, or remove it, by contacting us.
  • If you live in the EU, UK or another region with data-protection laws, you also have the rights those laws give you. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Legal basis (EU/UK)

We run the online records service because you asked for it by creating an account (contract). We moderate names and stop cheating because it is in our legitimate interest to keep the leaderboards fair and safe.

Retention

Account data is kept until you delete the account. If an account has no activity for 24 months, we may delete it. Server logs are kept for up to 30 days.

Children

The game asks your age before you create an account. Players under 13 cannot create one. Nothing is sent from their device. If you believe a child under 13 has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and say so in the game's release notes.
