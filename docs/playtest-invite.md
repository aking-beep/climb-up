# CLIMB UP playtest invite (copy and send)

For the 30–50 person playtest in `playtest-plan.md`. It describes the build
the testers get: the debug APK, or the web build for iPhones, with **no
records service set** (`climb_up/records/endpoint` is empty). If a later
build points at a live service, change the "Records" paragraph to match.

---

**Subject: Play-test CLIMB UP: a climbing game, about a week of short sessions**

Hi,

Thanks for helping test CLIMB UP. It's two climbing games in one:
- **Crag:** short sport routes, plus a new route every day.
- **Expedition:** Kilimanjaro with a team of four. You plan each day at
  camp and climb the big walls yourself.

**What we're asking**
- A 30-minute first session on a call. We'll mostly watch and ask you to
  think out loud.
- Then about a week of playing whenever you like.
- At the end, a short survey, and pasting your usage log (below).

**How to install**
- **Android:** open this link on your phone and install the APK: [link].
  Your phone will ask you to allow installs from this source. That's
  expected for a test build.
- **iPhone:** open this link in Safari and turn the phone sideways:
  [link]. There is no App Store build yet.

**Your records stay on your phone**
- Your best times, styles and high points, and the daily route's results,
  are kept on your phone only.
- The **Friends** and **Global** tabs on the records screen will say
  "not connected". That's expected: no online leaderboard is running in
  this test.
- Nothing you climb is sent anywhere.

**Your usage log stays on your phone too**
- The game keeps a short log of what you did (routes, falls, retries),
  only on your phone.
- At the end we'll ask you to copy it: **Settings → Copy my usage log
  (for playtests)**. Then paste it into the survey.
- You can turn it off in Settings at any time.

**If something goes wrong**, write to arclabs@arctransformationgroup.com
with:
- your phone model;
- what you were doing;
- a screenshot if you can.

Thanks,
ARC Labs
